How it works

  1. Complete your assessment

    Tell us about your hair loss, health history, and current medications.

  2. A clinician reviews your answers

    They evaluate your information and contact you if anything else is needed.

  3. Get your recommendation

    View the clinician’s treatment recommendation and next steps in your patient account.

  4. Receive your treatment

    If prescribed, treatment is delivered with instructions for use.

Your questions, answered.

What does the assessment involve?

You’ll answer questions about your hair loss, age, previous treatments, and current medications. You can also share other health information or questions.

Do I need to book an appointment?

Start with an online assessment. A clinician reviews your answers and may contact you for more information or a consultation if needed.

Will I definitely receive a prescription?

No. A clinician determines whether treatment is appropriate after reviewing your assessment. Completing an assessment does not guarantee a prescription.

What happens after the assessment?

A clinician reviews your information and recommends next steps. If treatment is prescribed, you can review the recommendation in your patient account before proceeding.
